Arkham House

A publishing house of horror and weird fiction—founded in 1939 to rescue Lovecraft from the pulps and keep the uncanny in print.

To enable uploads, hiding books, and other features, run the local server from the project folder:

npm run serve

Then open http://localhost:8742

Hover a cover to edit a book, hide it, or upload a custom cover image. Hidden books stay out of the gallery unless you turn on Show hidden.
